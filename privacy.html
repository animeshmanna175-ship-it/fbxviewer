Privacy Policy

FBX Viewer

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 • Package ID: com.aistudio.amberfbx.kqvrmx

Summary: FBX Viewer processes all 3D models, textures, renders, and video exports 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, analytics, or uploaded files.

1. Information We Collect

FBX Viewer does not collect, store on external servers, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, or crash telemetry. The app does not require user registration or sign-in and does not contain third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. Local File & Photo Access

To view 3D models, apply custom textures, and export renders, FBX Viewer uses Android's built-in privacy-preserving system pickers only when you explicitly choose a file:

  • 3D Model Files (FBX, OBJ, STL, PLY): Accessed via the Android Storage Access Framework (system file picker) solely when you select a file to open. Geometry is parsed locally in device memory.
  • Custom Photo Textures: Accessed via the zero-permission Android Photo Picker solely when you select an image to map onto a 3D model. The app never requests broad access to your photo library or storage.
  • Exports (MP4 Video, PNG/JPEG Images, FBX/OBJ/STL Meshes): Generated entirely on your device. Temporary export files are stored inside the app's private cache directory only as needed to save to your chosen destination or share through the Android system share sheet.

3. Local App Preferences

FBX Viewer stores minimal non-personal app settings (such as whether you have completed the initial onboarding walkthrough) locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

4. Device Permissions

FBX Viewer requests only the minimum permission required for its interactive experience:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly to provide optional tactile haptic feedback when interacting with buttons, sliders, and viewport controls. Haptics can be muted at any time inside the app.

FBX Viewer does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or broad external storage permissions.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Because FBX Viewer operates completely offline and collects no user data, no information is shared with third parties. When you choose to share an exported video, image, or 3D file via the Android Share Sheet, the selected file is handed directly to the destination app you pick under that app's own privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

FBX Viewer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if future versions of FBX Viewer introduce new features or permissions. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
